# Roadmap

- Add legal test samples for late-loading players and independent audio/video tracks, keeping per-case results in the compatibility matrix.
- Measure long-running resource use, low-disk recovery and the transfer ceiling on comparable historical and current builds.
- Improve how the extension represents adaptive audio tracks and associates multiple players with their manifests.
- Evaluate signed extension store distribution and an MCP Registry distribution method when packaging and account requirements can be verified.
